Driver Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if someone tries to take my reserved charger before I arrive? ▼

The physical charger is locked! The screen displays "Reserved for [Your Name / Pass ID]" and prompts for your secret 4-digit PIN. The charging cable will not unlatch until your phone scans the QR code or enters the PIN.

What if I get stuck in traffic and am late? ▼

You get a full 15-minute hold window. If you are delayed, you can tap "Extend Hold by 10 Minutes" in the reservation screen with a single touch.

Does VoltGrid work with my electric car? ▼

Yes! All VoltGrid superhubs feature standard CCS2 and Type 2 plugs, which are compatible with Tesla (with adapter), Tata, Hyundai, MG, Mahindra, Kia, BMW, and Porsche.
